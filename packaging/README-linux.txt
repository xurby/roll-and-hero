Roll & Hero for Linux (64-bit)
==============================

Start the game with:   ./start-roll-and-hero.sh
(or double-click start-roll-and-hero.sh in your file manager and choose "Run").

If your system can't run Chrome's sandbox, the script starts the game without
it; the game only shows its own page and your videos.

Settings are saved in ~/.config/Roll & Hero. Your video folders are remembered
between starts. To remove the game, delete this folder (and that settings folder).

Menu: press Alt to show it (Reload, Fullscreen, Zoom).
