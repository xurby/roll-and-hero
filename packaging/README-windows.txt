Roll & Hero for Windows
=======================

Start the game by double-clicking  roll-and-hero.exe
(Tip: right-click it > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut) to get a desktop icon.)

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app
isn't signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Everything stays in this folder plus your settings, which are saved in
%APPDATA%\Roll & Hero. Your video folders are remembered between starts.
To remove the game, delete this folder (and that settings folder if you like).

Menu: press Alt to show it (Reload, Fullscreen, Zoom).
